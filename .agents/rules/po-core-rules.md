# PO requirements rules

- Use the current request, `docs/context/product-context/discovery/entre-nous-product-discovery.md`, the exact BA brief, and their cited intake answers as product evidence. Event documents constrain the hackathon deliverable; research identifies questions, not approved product choices. The exploratory prototype is not a requirements source.
- Keep the source trail and unresolved questions visible. Context is a living source, not an approval gate. Do not convert a proposal, candidate acceptance criterion, or hypothesis into a confirmed requirement without an explicit decision.
- Separate the fictional demonstration from future real-person use. Do not import administrator archives, operational moderation, emergency response, or claims of mental-health benefit into the demo PRD merely because they appear in the later-service brief.
- Use an initial product PRD to inventory all discovery capabilities across labeled horizons. A scoped delivery PRD has one outcome and explicit in/out boundaries. Create additional scoped PRDs only when independently reviewable; link dependencies rather than duplicating requirements.
- For each requirement, state the actor, observable behaviour, source BR ID, discovery or answer reference, and acceptance evidence. If a needed behaviour is only proposed, label it `Hypothesis` and place the decision in open questions; do not make it a build commitment.
- Feature documents derive from an existing PRD. Keep stable feature and requirement IDs, reciprocal links, acceptance evidence, and coverage or explicit deferral. Do not turn a product-wide inventory into a build plan by grouping its items.
- Use `DRAFT` for an incomplete PRD, `BLOCKED` when a material contradiction or missing decision prevents a coherent scope, and `READY FOR REVIEW` when the PRD can be reviewed. `APPROVED` requires explicit user approval of that PRD, not separate approval of context files. Call the scope ready to build only when the PRD is accepted, material blockers are resolved, and the relevant architecture and validation plan are clear.
- Do not choose a stack, architecture, metric target, legal rule, safety procedure, or impact claim without source support. Mark gaps `Not provided.` and optional proposals `Hypothesis` with their rationale.
