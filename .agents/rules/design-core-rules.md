# Design rules

- Trace interface behaviour to the current product, experience, PRD, feature, and story sources. The landing and exploratory mockups may inform presentation but cannot create requirements.
- Keep the fictional hackathon demonstration distinct from a service for real participants. Do not imply active public support, therapy, guaranteed anonymity, or universal deletion.
- Distinguish owner-selected direction, observed artifact details, **Hypothesis** and `Not provided.`. Do not describe visual tokens as an approved brand system unless the owner explicitly selects them.
- Design the opener and joiner as temporary intentions, not fixed identities. Preserve reciprocal chat after joining and reflect who may end the exchange or leave.
- Separate hanging up an optional call from ending the exchange. Make waiting, closure and loss of access understandable from each participant's view.
- Give interface copy, focus order, readable contrast, reduced motion and responsive layouts concrete review attention. Record checks actually performed rather than claiming conformance.
- Keep mockups editable and linked from design context. Do not treat a simulated flow as a working LAN conversation or real audio.
