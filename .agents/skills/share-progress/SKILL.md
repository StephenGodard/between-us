# Share hackathon progress

The active role is `.agents/agents/build-in-public-agent.md`. Follow `.agents/rules/project-core-rules.md`. Read [the owner's editorial posture](references/editorial-posture.md) and [observed X posts](references/x-posts.md) when drafting. The posture is drawn from the owner's connected `CommunicationLinkedin.docx` and their AnimeSphere `journaliste.md` agent; consult the originals when available, especially for a substantial rewrite of this skill. Use them for voice and method, not as hackathon product evidence.

1. Identify the requested update and read the current evidence: relevant changed files, decisions, demos, screenshots, or the owner's supplied notes. For product claims, begin with `docs/context/README.md`, `docs/context/product-context/README.md`, and `docs/context/product-context/product-context.md`; follow their primary sources where a claim matters. Check `docs/context/hackathon-kickoff.md` for event facts.
2. Compare the candidate update with the observed posts. State what is genuinely new and avoid reposting the same Product Discovery announcement. Where the new development is a proposal or review, describe it in those terms.
3. Find the narrative behind the work: what question or friction led to it, what was actually done, what choice remains, and what a reader can learn. Draft in French and first person with a concrete opening and natural progression. Preserve the owner's words when quoting. Keep a single X post within the standard 280-character limit unless the owner requests a thread. Use `#parisdevdayhack` for hackathon posts; use event account tags only when relevant and correctly attributed. Keep emojis sparse, as in the observed posts.
4. Check every factual claim against its source and remove unverified outcome, impact, maturity, or safety claims. Do not expose private data, secrets, or sensitive fictional dialogue. Report any essential missing detail as `Not provided.` and an editorial suggestion as `Hypothesis`.
5. Present one post ready for review, plus a short source trail and any question whose answer would materially change the post. If the user asks for several options, provide meaningfully different angles. Post, schedule, reply, or message only when explicitly requested; after publishing, record the actual post URL in the source notes if the user wants continued tracking.
