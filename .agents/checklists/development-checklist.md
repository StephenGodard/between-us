# Development checklist

- [ ] The story and plan establish what changed; current product and technical context, `constraints.md`, `stack.md`, ADR and relevant engineering guides were read before coding. Deviations from the plan are explained.
- [ ] Client, backend and transient data behaviour match the accepted architecture and engineering guides.
- [ ] Meaningful affected checks ran; commands, outcomes and failures are recorded.
- [ ] Each acceptance criterion has observed evidence or an explicit gap; cross-device or audio claims use actual devices.
- [ ] Technical context and plan progress reflect verified changes; unresolved product or ADR decisions remain visible.
- [ ] No unrequested future-service feature or unsupported readiness claim was introduced.
