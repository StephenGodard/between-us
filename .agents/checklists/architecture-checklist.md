# Architecture checklist

- [ ] The technical context names exact functional sources, their material decisions and gaps, and the delivery scope.
- [ ] User needs, observed code, hypotheses, and approved decisions are distinguishable and cited.
- [ ] The local fictional demo and future real-person service are not conflated.
- [ ] Candidate options are compared against sourced needs and material tradeoffs; no stack or platform is silently inherited from the prototype.
- [ ] Data, identity, communication, privacy, safety, and operational boundaries are recorded or marked `Not provided.` where they affect the choice.
- [ ] An ADR exists only for a material decision; its status and approval evidence are accurate.
- [ ] The technical context tells downstream agents what they may rely on and what remains provisional or blocked.
- [ ] Unrun checks are marked `NOT RUN` with a reason; no test, cost, performance, compliance, or production claim is invented.
