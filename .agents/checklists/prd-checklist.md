# PRD checklist

- [ ] The PRD names its delivery outcome, status, exact discovery and brief paths, and both source statuses.
- [ ] Scope distinguishes the fictional hackathon demonstration from future real-person use, with explicit exclusions.
- [ ] Each confirmed requirement has an actor, observable behaviour, acceptance evidence, a BA BR ID, and discovery or intake evidence.
- [ ] Hypotheses, candidate BA checks, and unresolved decisions are not presented as approved build commitments.
- [ ] Requirements are mutually consistent, scoped to the PRD, and specific enough for later stories and implementation planning without choosing an unsupported stack.
- [ ] Nonfunctional needs, safety claims, metrics, and impact statements have source evidence or are marked `Not provided.`
- [ ] Source conflicts and later decisions are reconciled against the cited intake answer; missing checks are marked `NOT RUN` with a reason.
- [ ] `APPROVED` and build readiness are claimed only with explicit approval of this PRD, approved source artifacts, and resolved material blockers.
