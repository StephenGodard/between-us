# PRD checklist

- [ ] The PRD names its type, status, exact discovery and brief paths, and material source decisions or gaps; scoped PRDs name one delivery outcome.
- [ ] Scope distinguishes the fictional hackathon demonstration from future real-person use, with explicit exclusions.
- [ ] Each confirmed requirement has an actor, observable behaviour, acceptance evidence, discovery or intake evidence, and a BA BR ID where one exists; absent BR coverage is stated.
- [ ] Hypotheses, candidate BA checks, and unresolved decisions are not presented as approved build commitments.
- [ ] Requirements are mutually consistent, scoped to the PRD, and specific enough for later stories and implementation planning without choosing an unsupported stack.
- [ ] Nonfunctional needs, safety claims, metrics, and impact statements have source evidence or are marked `Not provided.`
- [ ] Source conflicts and later decisions are reconciled against the cited intake answer; missing checks are marked `NOT RUN` with a reason.
- [ ] `APPROVED` and build readiness are claimed only with explicit acceptance of this PRD and resolved material blockers; context files have no approval gate.
- [ ] An initial product PRD inventories discovery capabilities with stable IDs, source and decision state, horizon and coverage; it does not treat the inventory as an approved build scope.
