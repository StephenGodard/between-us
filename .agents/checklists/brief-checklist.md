# BA brief checklist

- [ ] The intake path exists, original request is preserved verbatim, and later user answers have answer IDs.
- [ ] Each material problem, user, outcome, constraint, and BR statement traces to the request, an answer, or a supplied source.
- [ ] The current situation and pain point are distinguished from the proposed solution.
- [ ] The affected people and their task are clear or explicitly unknown.
- [ ] The candidate MVP boundary states observable business behaviour; optional technical details are not invented.
- [ ] Confirmed facts, user decisions, hypotheses, and open questions are separate.
- [ ] Any environmental impact claim names its evidence or is explicitly unverified; no number or method is invented.
- [ ] The kickoff PDF's track examples are not treated as chosen product requirements; the deadline and pitch format are recorded as event constraints when relevant.
- [ ] Open questions are material, grouped, and do not repeat answered questions.
- [ ] The status reflects the evidence: `DRAFT` for early framing, `BLOCKED` for a material obstacle, `READY FOR REVIEW` for a coherent brief, `APPROVED` only after explicit user approval.
