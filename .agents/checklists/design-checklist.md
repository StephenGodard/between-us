# Design review checklist

- [ ] Sources and decision states are traceable; the landing's visual details are labeled as proposals unless selected by the owner.
- [ ] The mockup's scope, fictional demo boundary, participant perspectives and included states are clear.
- [ ] Entry, subject, waiting, reciprocal chat, optional call, joiner exit and opener closure are represented or their absence is recorded.
- [ ] Copy avoids unsupported care, privacy and deletion claims; temporary intentions do not become permanent labels.
- [ ] Layout, typography, contrast, keyboard focus, reduced motion and target device sizes have been reviewed or marked `NOT RUN` with a reason.
- [ ] Interactions demonstrated by a mockup are distinguished from real network, audio and persistence behaviour.
- [ ] Open design questions and feedback are reflected in the living design context; no unapproved product or architecture decision is implied.
