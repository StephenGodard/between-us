# Between Us — visual identity proposal

**Updated:** 24 October 2026  
**Status:** Hypothesis for review, not a final brand decision.

## Direction

The owner requested a direction inspired by [MindDay](https://www.mindday.com/), especially its color, typography and page construction. The reference uses a white canvas, strong rounded sans-serif headlines, turquoise emphasis, spacious sections and rounded product mockups. This proposal adapts those broad design principles to Between Us with its own conversation illustration, logo, palette and copy. MindDay's clinical claims, product imagery and brand assets are not used.

The landing page remains a presentation of a fictional citizen-to-citizen exchange, not a therapy or psychological-care service. Its hero messages are illustrative fiction, not the final grief script.

## Logo

- Two overlapping speech forms represent two distinct voices in a reciprocal exchange.
- The first form is turquoise; the second uses a dark outline. A lowercase bold wordmark gives the identity a friendly, contemporary tone.
- The favicon uses the speech forms on a turquoise ground.
- The English wordmark follows the owner's “between us” wording in the current request. The product context previously called “Entre nous” a working name; final naming remains open.

## Typography

- [Plus Jakarta Sans](https://fonts.google.com/specimen/Plus+Jakarta+Sans), locally hosted as a variable WOFF2 file under its [Open Font License](fonts/OFL.txt), is used for headlines, body and controls. Weights 400–800 make the hierarchy clear while keeping one family.
- Headlines are bold, short and closely tracked. Supporting copy uses a narrower measure and comfortable line height.
- The browser has system sans-serif fallbacks when the font cannot load.

## Composition

- A white, split hero introduces the concept and presents an original portrait phone mockup on a rounded aqua field. Its five-scene CSS loop follows only the opener's screen in the selected subject-led entry (variant A): choose to talk, type « Deuil », open the subject and wait, write in the connected chat, then end the exchange. The chat reproduces the mockup's perspective: the opener's sent message appears on the right under “Vous”, then the other person's response appears on the left. The closed scene hides all messages. The loop lasts twenty seconds, with eight seconds for chat. Five story-style bars fill from left to right as each scene plays, with completed bars remaining full until the loop resets. Reduced-motion settings show the conversation with completed bars.
- A light aqua section carries the product idea and three simple value cards.
- The journey uses four rounded cards with restrained yellow, lilac and aqua variation.
- The hero opens with the owner's « Ça reste entre nous. » to evoke a moment of confidence and the product name. One short paragraph combines the need to be heard with the subject-led encounter; the later broad statement panel carries the reciprocal “On est tous aidants” idea and its full explanation. The brand line expresses the intended tone; it does not settle the future service's administrator archive and confidentiality rules.
- Buttons use dark ink or dark teal with white text. Turquoise is primarily an accent, keeping small text readable.

## Copy boundaries

Favor “se parler”, “écouter”, “échanger”, “un moment” and “un sujet”. Do not promise therapeutic benefit, guaranteed anonymity, complete deletion or access to real listeners. The page identifies the hackathon demonstration as fictional and unavailable as a public support service.
