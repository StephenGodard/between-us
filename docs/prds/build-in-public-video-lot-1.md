## Goal and delivery boundary

Produce **lot 1: a short, chat-centered video** for Build in Public and a rapid product demonstration to present (A-39, A-41). It depicts the intended experience with fictional, acted participants (A-8 to A-11). Lot 1 precedes the working application; the video must not imply that unbuilt capabilities have already been verified. The real, unscripted session application and optional audio call are [lot 2](interactive-session-mvp.md) (A-13, A-38, A-39, A-41).

## People and journey

The viewer first sees an in-person listening moment, then a fictional remote exchange in the product concept (A-9). One person raises a conversation subject; another chooses to join it; both respond (A-28, A-31, A-32, A-38). Grief is the selected main fictional example (A-26), not a limit on the later application's subjects. The video communicates “on est tous aidants” and the participant-facing end of the exchange (A-11). Exact dialogue and shot sequence: **Not provided.**

## Scope

### In scope

- Storyboarded, scripted video using fictional people and messages, distinct from lot 2's unscripted application (A-8 to A-10, A-37 to A-39).
- In-person opening and transition to the remote product experience (A-9).
- Illustration of subject opening, subject choice, reciprocal written chat and participant-visible closure (A-11, A-28, A-31, A-32, A-38, A-41).
- A submission cut of two minutes, as required by the supplied hackathon source. Build in Public reuse is the owner's stated purpose (A-39); exact publication format is **Not provided**.

### Out of scope

- Implementing or accepting the server, live chat and real audio capabilities of lot 2.
- Depicting an audio call in the lot 1 video (A-41).
- Real support seekers, mental-health benefit claims, moderation, emergency response and a public service (A-8, A-10 to A-12).
- Publishing the video to a platform; destination and publication timing are **Not provided**.

## Nonfunctional and content needs

- The owner chose a warm, human atmosphere (A-25). Visual treatment, cast, voiceover, subtitles, aspect ratio and export settings: **Not provided.**
- The project aims to contribute to mental well-being, but no effect has been established (A-3, A-6); the video must not present that effect as a proven result.
- A-10 initially mentions showing an ephemeral call. A-41 refines the lot boundary: the video centers on written chat, while the audio call belongs to lot 2. The storyboard must therefore make chat opening, reciprocal messages and final closure legible without a call scene.
