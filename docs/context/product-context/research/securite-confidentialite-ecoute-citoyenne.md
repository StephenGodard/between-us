# BA research — safety, moderation and privacy for citizen-to-citizen listening

Research date: 24 October 2026  
Status: Preliminary research for the brief, not a legal decision or permission to launch  
Related brief: `docs/context/product-context/briefs/espaces-ecoute-ephemeres.md`

## Scope reviewed

The proposed service connects two citizens around a separation or bereavement. Their chat disappears from their views when closed and they cannot reconnect. The owner wants administrators to retain conversation history for moderation and is considering contact with emergency services in severe cases, preferably with the person's confirmation. The intended first real audience is adults in France (A-7). The hackathon uses acted, fictional exchanges (A-8), without real conversations with people in difficulty. These choices come from intake answers A-1 to A-8. The sources below identify external considerations; they do not change the owner's decisions.

## What the sources support

| Subject | Source-backed finding | Implication for the project |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Nature of the data | The CNIL defines health data broadly, including mental health information even outside a care pathway. Some messages may therefore qualify, depending on their contents. [CNIL — What is health data?](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/quest-ce-ce-quune-donnee-de-sante) | Treat the archive as potentially highly sensitive; classify the actual data collected. |
| Legal grounds | Processing sensitive data requires an Article 6 GDPR legal basis **and** an Article 9 condition. Explicit consent is possible but not an automatic answer for every purpose. [CNIL — Legal bases](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/les-bases-legales/liceite-essentiel-sur-les-bases-legales); [CNIL — Article 9](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/reglement-europeen-protection-donnees/chapitre2) | Define the purposes of the exchange, moderation archive, reports and any disclosure to emergency services separately before choosing grounds. |
| Anonymity | A pseudonym or an identity hidden from the other participant is not necessarily anonymization under the GDPR, especially if exchanges are archived. [CNIL — Anonymization](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/technologies/lanonymisation-de-donnees-personnelles) | Promise “identity concealed from other participants” only where true, and explain what administrators retain and can see. |
| Retention | Data cannot be kept indefinitely; the period must be justified by its purpose. The CNIL provides no universal period for these chats. [CNIL — Retention periods](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/passer-laction/les-durees-de-conservation-des-donnees); [CNIL — Health and well-being apps](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/applications-mobiles-en-sante-et-protection-des-donnees-personnelles-les-questions-se-poser) | Test whether archiving **every** chat is necessary and proportionate. Distinguish ordinary from reported chats, and define justified periods and deletion rules rather than inventing a number of days. |
| Access and security | The CNIL recommends limited permissions and access logging; people must be informed of recipients and retention periods. [CNIL — Permissions](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/securite-gerer-les-habilitations); [CNIL — Operation logs](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/securite-tracer-les-operations); [CNIL — Information](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/traitement-de-donnees-de-sante-comment-informer-les-personnes-concernees) | Define roles, reasons to access an archive, logs and information shown before a chat; administrators should not freely browse every conversation. |
| Individual rights | Hiding a conversation in the interface does not erase rights concerning retained data. Access, correction, erasure and restriction apply under their respective conditions. [CNIL — Information and rights](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/traitement-de-donnees-de-sante-comment-informer-les-personnes-concernees) | Provide a way to contact the data controller after closure while protecting the other participant's data. |
| Impact assessment | A data protection impact assessment (DPIA; AIPD in French) is required for processing likely to create high risk to rights and freedoms. Sensitive data and vulnerable people are relevant criteria; assessment precedes processing. [CNIL — DPIA](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/ce-quil-faut-savoir-sur-lanalyse-dimpact-relative-la-protection-des-donnees-aipd) | Prepare an assessment before real use given the possible combination of sensitive chats, archiving and distressed users; confirm the formal requirement against the final scope. |
| Danger and consent | Consent is not always the only processing ground, including where vital interests are at stake. French criminal law provides, under conditions, for assistance to a person in imminent danger and reporting some violence against minors or people unable to protect themselves. [CNIL — Is consent always required?](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/cnil-direct/question/reglement-europeen-le-consentement-est-il-obligatoire); [Service-Public — Failure to assist](https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F34551); [Légifrance — Article 434-3](https://www.legifrance.gouv.fr/codes/article_lc/LEGIARTI000037289453) | The owner's preference for confirmation in ordinary cases cannot be promised as an exceptionless rule. Define serious-danger cases with qualified legal and crisis advice. |
| Service classification | The European Commission uses private WhatsApp messaging as an example distinct from public online-platform features under the DSA; other obligations may still apply depending on the final functions. [European Commission — WhatsApp and DSA](https://digital-strategy.ec.europa.eu/en/news/commission-designates-whatsapp-very-large-online-platform-under-digital-services-act) | Do not assume every social-network obligation applies to a private chat; reassess if public topics or content are added. |
| Hosting and medical purpose | French HDS hosting certification depends on the context in which health data is collected. Medical-device software classification depends on the stated intended purpose; simple messaging or a non-medical well-being app does not automatically qualify. [ANS — HDS](https://esante.gouv.fr/produits-services/hds); [MDCG — Software qualification guidance](https://health.ec.europa.eu/document/download/b45335c5-1679-4c71-a91c-fc7a4d37f12b_en?filename=md_mdcg_2019_11_guidance_qualification_classification_software_en.pdf) | Have the service and public wording reviewed before selecting hosting or claiming to treat a condition. “Initial emotional support between citizens” better describes the current sourced intent. |

## Candidate procedure before a real-world test

This is a **working hypothesis**, not a complete legal requirement or safety guarantee.

1. **Before the exchange:** Explain that the service is not an emergency response; participants' civil identities are concealed from each other; the chat ends for them at closure; and administrators retain history for stated purposes. Request separate, understandable agreement wherever a particular processing activity requires it. Define the listener's role as listening, sharing experience and offering suggestions only if welcome, without an outcome promise.
2. **During the exchange:** Let either person leave and report easily. Prevent new contact after closure. Do not leave the listener solely responsible for classifying serious danger or securing care.
3. **Ordinary report:** Create a moderation case, assign a human owner, restrict access to necessary material, decide and record the outcome. Tell participants how reports and archives work.
4. **Potentially immediate danger:** Show a route to appropriate emergency services and refer the case to an authorized person under a defined procedure. A platform-initiated call requires reliable enough information to act. Asking confirmation and establishing a legal ground for disclosure are separate questions. In France, 15 is medical emergency assistance and 17 is police/gendarmerie. [Service-Public — Emergency numbers](https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/actualites/A17758)
5. **After closure:** No renewed contact between participants. Separate how long they can see the conversation from administrator retention; apply deletion and individual-rights procedures. A data breach presenting risk to people's rights must be documented and, depending on its severity, reported to the CNIL within applicable deadlines. [CNIL — Data breaches](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/violations-de-donnees-personnelles-les-regles-suivre)

## Questions requiring specialist validation

- Exact classification of messages as health data, legal grounds for each processing activity, any HDS requirement, access and erasure rights for an invisible chat, and a documented retention period.
- Exact DSA and French LCEN scope depending on whether topics are public or only private conversations exist.
- Emergency protocol, reporting duties and limits of consent in serious cases, especially if minors are ever admitted.
- Operational moderation and referral capacity before a pilot with people actually experiencing difficulties.

General sources alone cannot determine the legal status of a service whose features and organization are not yet fixed.

## Resolution plan before any real exchange

This plan turns the brief's open questions into reviewable deliverables. These are **BA proposals**; legal classifications and crisis responses require review by the listed professionals.

| Order | Work and proposed owner | Deliverable and decision |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Hackathon demo | Project owner | Scripted **two-minute** video with two adults and fictional messages and conversation. Show chat activation, messages, a short-lived call and the end of participant contact; optionally simulate a report. Do not imply that disappearance from a screen means guaranteed deletion everywhere. No real emergency calls or distressed person's data. The two-minute requirement comes from the [event document](../../hackathon-kickoff.md); A-8 to A-10 define the acted exchange and proposed interactions. |
| 2. Data map | Owner with GDPR counsel or DPO | One record per purpose: matching, conversation, ordinary archive, report, emergency response and access log. For each, state data collected, who can access it, recipients, Article 6 ground, any Article 9 condition, retention and deletion trigger. Record the processing where required. [CNIL — Processing register](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/RGPD-le-registre-des-activites-de-traitement) |
| 3. Archive decision | Owner with GDPR counsel or DPO and moderation lead | A matrix for ordinary, reported and severe-danger cases: justification for full or reduced retention, reasoned periods, restricted access, access logs, deletion and rights after closure. Describe “invisible to participants” separately from “deleted everywhere.” [CNIL — Retention periods](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/passer-laction/les-durees-de-conservation-des-donnees) |
| 4. Safety and moderation | Moderation lead and deputy, with safety specialist | Conduct rules for both roles, reporting route, authorized reviewers, operating hours, internal response times, interruption and escalation criteria, decision log, incident and breach procedures. Review archive access and test fictional cases of hurtful messages, harassment and identity disclosure. [CNIL — Permissions](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/securite-gerer-les-habilitations); [CNIL — Breaches](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/violations-de-donnees-personnelles-les-regles-suivre) |
| 5. Emergency protocol | Crisis specialist, lawyer and moderation lead | A decision tree exercised on fictional cases: ordinary support, distress needing referral, possibly immediate danger. Define who decides, what reliable identity/location information is available, what the user sees, and when disclosure may or must occur. Separate requesting confirmation, the legal ground for disclosure and any duty to assist. Do not promise an exceptionless consent-only call rule. [Service-Public — Failure to assist](https://www.service-public.fr/particuliers/vosdroits/F34551); [CNIL — Consent](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/cnil-direct/question/reglement-europeen-le-consentement-est-il-obligatoire) |
| 6. Review before pilot | GDPR counsel or DPO with owner | Confirm the service's status regarding health data, HDS, any medical purpose and DSA scope; review the privacy notice and individual rights; perform a DPIA if high risk is confirmed. The CNIL does not automatically approve a project; prior consultation may apply if high residual risk remains. [CNIL — DPIA](https://www.cnil.fr/fr/ce-quil-faut-savoir-sur-lanalyse-dimpact-relative-la-protection-des-donnees-aipd); [GDPR — Article 36](https://cnil.fr/fr/reglement-europeen-protection-donnees/chapitre4) |
| 7. Limited pilot | Owner, moderation team and pilot evaluator | Admit only volunteering adults in France after the preceding decisions are signed off and procedures rehearsed. Check participants' understanding, real reporting capacity, whether they felt heard and any negative effects. Stop the pilot if the safety protocol cannot be met. No benefit rate or well-being guarantee is presumed. |

**Proposed gate to real exchanges (BA hypothesis):** No real participant until deliverables 2–6 are written, reviewed by their owners and rehearsed using fictional cases. Work on the scripted demo can continue during that review.
